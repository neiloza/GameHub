## GUGALANNA — THE BULL OF HEAVEN

Ishtar demanded him from her father Anu after Gilgamesh refused her. Anu warned
her that loosing the bull would bring seven years of famine to the land, and she
took him anyway. He came down and drank the Euphrates dry in a single draught,
and every snort of his opened a pit that swallowed hundreds of men. Enkidu and
Gilgamesh killed him — and the gods took Enkidu's life for it.

**Typing.** Dark/Air is weak to Fire, Grass, Electric, and Light, and resists
Water and Psychic. Fighting hits it for a quarter, because Dark and Air both
resist it — the only quarter-resistance on the roster and the reason Gugalanna
can sit in front of Sun Wukong, Fenrir, or Roc and simply refuse to die. It has
no quadruple weakness at all. Four two-times weaknesses is a lot of exposure,
but none of them compound, which matters more.

**Seven Years of Famine.** No healing on either side while it is on the field.
Not reduced, not halved — off. This is the roster's only global lock and the
only ability that turns its own team off along with the enemy's, so bringing it
means building around the drought rather than dropping it into any team. Its job
is to answer Light, which is tied for the largest type in the game and has spent
this whole design accumulating recovery: Gift, the full-team restore, Sanctuary,
Revival Blessing, the lifesteal attack. Nothing has been allowed to stop that
since Heal Block came out of Dark. Now something does.

It also fixes the last structural complaint on the roster. Air was four birds
and nothing else. It is now three birds and a bull.

### Rulings Seven Years of Famine needs

These are my recommendations, not decisions — say the word on any you want
flipped.

- **Fields.** Overgrowth stops healing. Sanctuary stops healing on switch-in.
  Both fields still run out their five turns; they simply do nothing while the
  Gugalanna is out.
- **Abilities.** Photosynthesis still restores mana but no HP. Hearthfire and
  Regenerator do nothing.
- **Draining moves.** Sap and the Light lifesteal attack still deal their full
  damage. The user just gets nothing back.
- **Revival is not healing.** Revival Blessing and Rebirth both work. A revived
  character returns at whatever HP the effect specifies and cannot then be
  topped up. Miracle also works, because surviving at 1 HP is not a heal.
- **It stops the instant Gugalanna leaves.** Same as every other in-play effect
  in the game. The drought is Gugalanna standing there, not a field.

## Shelved: the spider

Grass/Dark, with an ability that traps whatever it attacks. It was a real
contender for the last slot and it is worth keeping on the shelf, because it is
the only invertebrate anyone proposed and the roster has none.

Two things to know if it ever comes back in. Grass/Dark is quadruple weak to
Fire, and Fire is a five-character type holding Eruption — that character gets
one window per match and no second chances. And a trap-on-hit ability stacks
with Kelpie's Undertow into a lock that has no counterplay except Cyclone or
Tempest, so the trap would need to end when the spider leaves, exactly like
Bind does.

Fenrir is the character it would replace. It is the only swap that keeps every
type at four or five, and Fenrir is the third canine on a roster with no
arachnid at all.

## What is still open

1. **Forewarning's scale.** Reveal the active opponent's moves, or the whole
   opposing team's? The team-wide version fits the myth — Matsya warned of a
   flood that would drown the world, not a puddle — but it deletes hidden
   information from the game in one ability.
2. **Death Touch has no cost.** It defeats a character outright, and Grim
   Reaper's whole ability is making it cheaper. Mana alone may not be enough of
   a brake.
3. **Taunt may be mandatory.** It shuts off five of the seven neutral moves,
   plus every heal and every setup move in the game. A move that good on a
   roster this size is not a choice.
4. **Mono-types need compensating stats.** Dual typing is now the default,
   sixteen to nine. The core nine give up coverage for nothing unless their raw
   numbers are better.
5. **The fifth stat, the damage formula, and the stat budgets.** All still
   untouched. This is the next real body of work.
