# Animas — Character Stat Spreads

Twenty-five stat lines, organised by type. Every one is built on the same rule:
**each character does one degenerate thing as well as it can possibly be done,
and is genuinely bad at everything else.**

---

## The design philosophy, stated plainly

**Balance here is not the absence of broken things. It is the presence of
twenty-five of them.** Kelpie should drown people who cannot escape, because that
is the fantasy of a kelpie. The Grim Reaper should delete characters outright,
because that is the fantasy of Death. Every character should have a line on their
sheet that makes a player say "wait, it can just do that?"

What stops it being chaos is the second half. **The price of a degenerate
strength is a genuinely crippling weakness**, real enough that a mistimed switch
loses you the character outright. The counterplay is never "their Ability is not
that strong." It is **kill it before it does the thing, or bring the one
character that answers it.**

Do not fix a strong interaction by weakening an Ability. Fix it by making the
character that holds it easier to punish.

## How the four stats read

**HP** is an index. **Hit points = HP stat × 2.5**, shown in brackets throughout.

**Power** is the damage multiplier ÷ 100. Roc's 170 multiplies every move by 1.7;
Baku's 20 multiplies it by 0.2.

**Speed** is turn order, high first.

**Mana** is the pool, and **100 is the wall** — at or above it a character can use
its type's signature move, below it never.

**HP and Speed follow the creature; Power and Mana do the balancing.** A player
looking at a world-turtle expects it to be unkillable and glacial, and it is.

## The extremes

| | Lowest | Highest | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HP** | Xiuhcoatl — 20 *(50 hp)* | World Turtle — 250 *(625 hp)* | **12.5×** |
| **Power** | Matsya, Unicorn — 30 | Otso, Roc — 150 | **5.0×** |
| **Speed** | World Turtle — 10 | Raiju — 150 | **15×** |
| **Mana** | Hydra, Phoenix, Roc — 50 | Baku — 200 | **4.0×** |

Totals run 275 to 460, mean 381. **Fourteen of twenty-five clear the
hundred-mana wall.**

**HP and Speed are still the polarized stats; Power and Mana have compressed.**
Power now spans 5× where it was 8.5×, and Mana 4× where it was 10×. That is a
real change of character — the roster reads less like nine wildly different
economies and more like everyone being roughly able to afford things. Not
necessarily wrong, but it happened gradually and is worth deciding on rather than
drifting into. The two stats you named as the balancing levers are now the two
doing the least work.

**Two characters can choose between signature moves.** Bakeneko at 120 Mana
reaches Pyroclasm *or* Mind Crush; **Otso at 100 now reaches Bloom *or* Frenzy.**
Nothing else on the roster gets that choice, and it is a genuinely distinctive
thing for a dual type to have.

## What the recent changes fixed and broke

### Fixed — Leshy is no longer a worse World Turtle

Eighty Power against World Turtle's forty is a real difference, and 100 Mana
against 160 is a real trade. Leshy is the mid-weight wall that can actually
threaten something; World Turtle is the immovable one that cannot. The dominance
problem is closed.

### Fixed — Roc is no longer stranded

At 50 Mana it can at least reach its cheap tools. Dropping Power from 170 to 150
also means **Otso and Roc now share the highest Power in the game**, which reads
correctly — a bear that has eaten and a bird that carries elephants.

### Broken — Kitsune stopped being a fox

At 90 Speed it is slower than Simargl, Bakeneko, Fenrir, Sun Wukong and
Thunderbird. **Illusion breaks the instant anything connects**, so a slow Kitsune
gets hit on the turn it arrives and the Ability does nothing at all. Its 130 Power
is now the third-highest in the game, on 125 hit points, at middling Speed — that
is a glass cannon, not a trickster.

**This is the one change I would push back on.** Either Kitsune goes back above
130 Speed and keeps Illusion meaningful, or Illusion should change to something
a slow character can use. A nine-tailed fox that gets outrun by a bear is the
wrong shape.

### Watch — Unicorn at 50 Speed

Miracle triggers from full health, so Unicorn wants to arrive clean and act
before anything touches it. At 50 Speed it now acts after most of the roster,
which means it usually takes a hit first and is no longer at full health when it
matters. The 150 Mana is fine; the Speed works against the Ability.

## Remaining collisions

**Simargl 90/80/110/90 and Fenrir 80/80/110/90** are still ten points apart and
still want opposite things — Berserk wants a fragile body with high Power,
Hearthfire wants a durable one. Recommend **Fenrir → 80/110/130/40** and
**Simargl → 120/80/90/80**.

**Bakeneko 80/90/110/120 and Garuda 80/100/120/120** are twenty apart. Recommend
lifting Garuda's Speed to 130 or 140, since First Flight is a tempo Ability and
Metronome is not.

## Open items

1. **Power scales damage only**, never healing or percentage effects.
2. **Stat modification needs a cap** — recommend double the base value from all
   sources combined.
3. **Speed ties.** Kelpie and Hydra at 30; Bakeneko, Simargl, Thunderbird and
   Fenrir all at 110. The tiebreak cannot be random — cleanest is that the
   defender acts first.
4. **Phoenix's 105 Speed** is the only stat off the multiple-of-ten grid.
   Harmless, but an exception rather than a slip.
5. **This roster needs a turn limit.**
