# Animas — Character Stat Spreads

Twenty-five stat lines, organised by type. Every one is built on the same rule:
**each character does one degenerate thing as well as it can possibly be done,
and is genuinely bad at everything else.**

---

## The design philosophy, stated plainly

**Balance here is not the absence of broken things. It is the presence of
twenty-five of them.** Kelpie should drown people who cannot escape, because that
is the fantasy of a kelpie. The Grim Reaper should delete characters outright,
because that is the fantasy of Death. Every character should have a line on their
sheet that makes a player say "wait, it can just do that?"

What stops it being chaos is the second half. **The price of a degenerate
strength is a genuinely crippling weakness**, real enough that a mistimed switch
loses you the character outright. The counterplay is never "their Ability is not
that strong." It is **kill it before it does the thing, or bring the one
character that answers it.**

Do not fix a strong interaction by weakening an Ability. Fix it by making the
character that holds it easier to punish.

## How the four stats read

**HP** is an index. **Hit points = HP stat × 2.5**, shown in brackets throughout.

**Power** is the damage multiplier ÷ 100. Roc's 170 multiplies every move by 1.7;
Baku's 20 multiplies it by 0.2.

**Speed** is turn order, high first.

**Mana** is the pool, and **100 is the wall** — at or above it a character can use
its type's signature move, below it never.

**HP and Speed follow the creature; Power and Mana do the balancing.** A player
looking at a world-turtle expects it to be unkillable and glacial, and it is.

## The extremes

| | Lowest | Highest | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HP** | Xiuhcoatl — 20 *(50 hp)* | World Turtle — 250 *(625 hp)* | **12.5×** |
| **Power** | Baku — 20 | Roc — 170 | **8.5×** |
| **Speed** | World Turtle — 10 | Bakeneko — 160 | **16×** |
| **Mana** | Roc — 20 | Baku — 200 | **10×** |

Totals run 275 to 460, mean 388.

**Xiuhcoatl at 50 hit points is the most extreme line in the game**, and there is
a symmetry worth keeping: Cometfall deals exactly 50 damage on switch-in, so the
serpent hits for precisely as much as it can survive. Anything at all kills it.
It exists to enter, deal 50, and leave — and at 140 Speed it usually can.

**Every hard hitter is still locked out of its own signature.** Roc, Otso,
Gugalanna, Hydra, Thunderbird and Bakeneko all sit at 60 Mana or below. The four
with the most mana — Baku at 20 Power, Matsya at 30, Unicorn at 30, World Turtle
at 40 — cannot hurt anybody at all.

## Two problems in the current spread

### Simargl and Fenrir are the same character

| | HP | Power | Speed | Mana |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Simargl** | 90 | 80 | 110 | 90 |
| **Fenrir** | 80 | 80 | 110 | 90 |

**Ten points of HP apart, and identical on the other three.** These are the only
two lines on the roster that collide, and they collide completely.

They also want opposite things. **Berserk doubles Fenrir's Power below half
health**, so Fenrir wants a Power worth doubling and a body that reaches the
threshold quickly. **Hearthfire heals Simargl off every attack it lands**, so
Simargl wants to be bulky enough to profit from staying in. Right now neither
gets what it needs, and they read as the same card.

**Recommend pulling them apart on the axis their Abilities care about:**

- **Fenrir → 80 HP · 110 Power · 130 Speed · 40 Mana** *(360)* — fragile and fast,
  reaching Berserk in one hit and swinging at 220 Power when it does.
- **Simargl → 120 HP · 80 Power · 90 Speed · 80 Mana** *(370)* — the bruiser that
  outlasts, healing off a body that can take the return fire.

### Leshy is a worse World Turtle

| | HP | Power | Speed | Mana | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leshy** | 150 *(375)* | 40 | 20 | 120 | 330 |
| **World Turtle** | 250 *(625)* | 40 | 10 | 160 | 460 |

Same Power. Nearly the same Speed. World Turtle has 100 more HP and 40 more Mana.
**Leshy's only advantage on the entire line is ten points of Speed**, and stat
totals are not a resource the player spends — every team slot costs the same, so
a dominated character is simply a worse pick.

Photosynthesis differentiates them in play, but not enough: 37 hit points and 12
mana a turn against 625 hit points and Unaware.

**Recommend making Leshy the healing wall rather than the bulk wall** — it should
be the one that never stops recovering, not the one that has the most to recover.
Something like **120 HP · 40 Power · 20 Speed · 170 Mana** *(350)* gives it the
highest-but-one Mana in the game, a real claim of its own, and leaves the raw
bulk record where it belongs.

## Open items

1. **Power scales damage only**, never healing or percentage effects. Otherwise
   Matsya at 30 Power becomes the worst healer in the game.
2. **Stat modification needs a cap.** Recommend a hard ceiling at double the base
   value from all sources combined.
3. **Speed ties.** Kelpie and Hydra are both at 30; Leshy and World Turtle are at
   20 and 10. The tiebreak cannot be random — cleanest is that the defender acts
   first.
4. **Phoenix's 105 Speed is the only stat off the multiple-of-ten grid.** Harmless,
   since Speed feeds turn order and nothing else, but worth noting as an exception
   rather than a slip.
5. **This roster needs a turn limit.** World Turtle at 625 hit points against a
   healing Leshy is a match with no ending.
