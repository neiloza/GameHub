# Animas — Speculative Tier List

Best guesses, no playtesting. **The mana rules changed everything**, and this list
is mostly a story about that: nothing regenerates in play, a switch refunds half
your maximum, and so the whole game is now about how many paid actions a
character gets before it has to give up the board.

---

## S — the five that warp a match

### Roc 🌪️/👊 · 110/150/110/50
**The best damage line in the game belongs to the only Air/Fighting character.**
Jet Stream is free and lifts Roc's whole moveset a tier, which drags Wind-Up from
−1 to ordinary speed. Turn one Jet Stream, turn two **Wind-Up for 375** — enough
to one-shot twenty of the twenty-five, for 25 mana.

Nine characters are fast enough to break it by hitting Roc first. **Against the
other fifteen it simply lands**, and it lands again every time Roc rebuilds.
That is a two-turn loop that deletes most of the roster.

### The Grim Reaper 🌑 · 60/90/45/130
Instant death is the strongest effect in the game and the Reaper gets it twice —
130 mana against a Harvest-halved Death Touch of 50, with a switch refunding 65
for a third. **No stat line answers "you are simply gone."**

It pays properly: 150 hit points, 45 Speed, dies to a single powerful attack. But
it only has to survive one turn per kill, and a two-for-one on a 310-total
character is a trade you take every time.

### Baku 🔮 · 130/70/25/190
**Pressure got far stronger when mana stopped regenerating.** It used to tax a
pool that refilled ten a turn. Now it halves the total number of paid actions the
opponent gets for as long as Baku stands there, and the only way to recover is to
switch — which is exactly what Baku wants, because the opponent leaves and their
plan resets.

Add 190 of its own mana and 325 hit points. It deals twelve damage a hit and it
does not care.

### Kelpie 💧 · 120/90/50/160
Undertow means the target cannot leave. Drown cannot be cleared by anything. That
is **a guaranteed kill unless the opponent removes Kelpie within three turns**,
and at 300 hit points and 90 Power that is not a given. 160 mana funds one Drown
immediately and a second after one switch cycle.

*Conditional on a ruling: if Drown clears when the target switches out, and the
opponent can kill Kelpie to free them, this drops to B. If Drown persists — which
the "immune to everything" wording implies — Kelpie is S.*

### Raiju ⚡ · 70/90/175/110
The fastest character in the game and **the only one that gains meaningful mana
while standing still.** Battery restores 27 a turn against a 20-mana Voltage that
doubles without limit: 50, 100, 200, 400, 800, with the meter rising the whole
time and every doubling landing first.

Four uncontested turns kills anything. 175 hit points means it rarely gets four —
but the opponent has to spend a turn answering it, every time, and that is its
own kind of pressure.

## A — strong, with a real condition attached

**Matsya** 💧/✨ · 100/30/85/200 — the biggest pool in the game, and under the new
economy that is the whole point. Renewal, Gift, Resurrection, and 100 refunded on
every switch. A team with Matsya does not lose characters permanently. Thirty
Power means it never threatens anything, which is why it is A and not S.

**Sphinx** 🔮/🌑 · 100/120/60/150 — Enigma stops repeats, which under hidden
movesets forces the opponent to reveal their whole hand. Pair it with 120 Power
and Mind Crush, and Sphinx is the only lock character that also kills you.

**Otso** 🌿/👊 · 120/150/70/100 — Honey-Gorge to 300 Power, Haymaker for 360, and
100 mana buys Bloom or Frenzy. Held back only by 70 Speed and by telegraphing the
charge turn to the entire board.

**Hydra** 💧/🌿 · 160/130/40/50 — 400 hit points, 130 Power, and switching out now
returns half its health *and* half its mana. Extremely hard to remove, and 50
mana is enough because it barely needs paid moves.

**Gugalanna** 🌑/🌪️ · 140/120/95/60 — 350 hit points, 120 Power, and Famine turns
off Matsya, Unicorn, Leshy and World Turtle entirely. A hard counter to a quarter
of the roster that also hits like a truck.

**World Turtle** 🌿/✨ · 200/20/5/160 — 500 hit points and Unaware. Nearly
unkillable, the best Inversion partner ever designed, and completely incapable of
winning on its own. **Its tier depends entirely on whether a turn limit exists.**

**Fafnir** 🔥 · 150/90/75/120 — 375 hit points bought specifically to survive the
three turns Slow Burn needs, then 180 Power and a 540-damage Pyroclasm. The
opponent's answer is simply to switch away for three turns, which is why it sits
here rather than higher.

## B — good, situational, or held back by one number

**Garuda** 🌪️ · 80/100/155/120 — very fast with Tempest in hand, but 200 hit
points and no way to threaten a kill on its own.

**Loch Ness** 💧/🌑 · 150/90/90/100 — a free untouchable turn on *every* entry,
and the new economy pays it to keep cycling. A quietly excellent pivot.

**Sun Wukong** 👊 · 90/110/145/90 — Bloodlust snowballs to 275 Power at three
knockouts. Needs the first one, and 225 hit points at 90 mana is a thin runway.

**Leshy** 🌿 · 120/80/15/100 — 80 sustain a turn makes it a genuine wall, but 15
Speed and 80 Power mean it never closes a match. **Drops to C if the sustain cap
lands.**

**Airavata** 👊/⚡ · 170/90/30/80 — 425 hit points and four doubled moves, but 30
Speed and 80 mana keep it below the wall and out of its own signature.

**Unicorn** ✨ · 100/30/105/150 — survives once, revives once, threatens never.

**Kitsune** 🔮/✨ · 50/130/135/70 — 130 Power at 135 Speed is a real threat, but
125 hit points and an Ability that breaks on contact make it a one-window
character.

**Simargl** 🔥/✨ · 110/80/100/90 — the roster's only generalist. Nothing it does
is bad and nothing it does is scary.

---

## C — needs something to go right

**Fenrir** 🌑/👊 · 90/110/130/40 — 220 Power under Berserk is genuinely
frightening, and **40 mana is the lowest in the game with no regeneration.** It
buys one Haymaker per switch cycle. The Ability is S-tier and the economy is not.

**Thunderbird** ⚡/🌪️ · 60/110/115/70 — Storm Front doubles everyone's mana, which
in a no-regen game is enormous — **and it hands the same gift to the opponent.**
The one Ability on the roster that helps both players equally.

**Xiuhcoatl** 🔥/⚡ · 30/80/165/90 — 165 Speed, 50 free damage on every entry, 45
mana refunded on every exit. A real chip engine, on **75 hit points that die to
literally anything.**

**Phoenix** 🔥/🌪️ · 40/90/125/50 — two lives of 100 hit points each and 50 mana.
Two short lives is still two short lives.

**Bakeneko** 🔥/🔮 · 80/50/120/120 — Metronome is fun and **50 Power means even a
perfect roll hits softly.** A Pyroclasm from Bakeneko deals 150.

## Three rulings would redraw this list

**Does Drown survive the target switching out?** If yes, Kelpie is S. If no, it is
B and the Water lock stops existing.

**Does per-turn healing cap at the largest source?** If yes, Leshy falls from B to
C and World Turtle loses much of its case. If no, both stay walls no median
attacker can break.

**Is there a turn limit?** World Turtle, Leshy, Matsya and Hydra are all built to
not lose. Without a clock they are far better than this list says. With one they
are exactly where they sit.

## What the tiers say about the design

**The S tier is five completely different win conditions** — a two-turn deletion
loop, two instant kills, an economic lock, an inescapable drowning, and an
infinite escalation. Not one of them is "hits hard." That is the design working.

**The C tier is four characters held back by mana, not by power.** Fenrir,
Xiuhcoatl, Phoenix and Thunderbird all have real Abilities and 40–70 mana in a
game where nothing refills in play. **If the tiers need flattening, that is the
lever** — ten or twenty points of Mana each, not a stat or an Ability rewrite.
