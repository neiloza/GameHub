About Liberty

Liberty is a resource for people who want to stay engaged with local, state, and federal government but don't have time to track every committee hearing and floor vote. Enter your ZIP, swipe through what's up for a vote, and make your position — support or oppose — visible to the people who represent you.

How it works

  1. Enter your ZIP. Liberty resolves it to your city and state.
  2. Browse by level — Town, County, State, Federal — one bill at a time. Mark each Interested (save it) or Not interested (skip it) and keep cycling through the pile.
  3. Read up on anything you saved, then hit Make My Voice Heard to record your support or opposition and, optionally, email your representatives directly.
  4. Get involved beyond bills — see local conventions, marches, and candidate races on the Events & Candidates tab.

About this data

This is a real, manually-researched snapshot — not a live feed. Every bill, event, and candidate entry links to its source. There's no backend, so:

  • Your saved bills and recorded votes live in this browser's localStorage — nothing is sent to a server.
  • Coverage is currently seeded for federal legislation; California, New York, and Texas state legislation; and San Francisco, CA and Austin, TX local legislation. Entering a ZIP outside that will still show federal (and state, if we've covered your state) — anything else gets logged locally as a requested region for the next research pass.

To go live with continuously updating data, wire data/repository.js up to:

  • Congress.gov API for federal bills
  • Open States API for state legislation
  • Your city or county's legislative system (Legistar, Municode, CivicPlus, etc.) for local ordinances
  • Google Civic Information API for precise representative lookups by address
