About Liberty

Liberty is a resource for people who want to stay engaged with local, state, and federal government but don't have time to track every committee hearing and floor vote. Browse what's up for a vote, read a plain-language summary, and make your position — support or oppose — visible to the people who represent you.

How it works

  1. Browse bills by level of government, topic, or keyword.
  2. Sign or start a petition stating your support or opposition, with an optional comment.
  3. Send your voice — Liberty drafts a message to the relevant representatives that you can send by email in one click.

About this data

This build ships with a curated set of sample bills and representatives so the app is fully explorable offline. Petitions and signatures you create are saved locally in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent to a server. To go live with real legislation, wire data/bills.js up to:

  • Congress.gov API for federal bills
  • Open States API for state legislation
  • Your city or county's legislative system (Legistar, Municode, CivicPlus, etc.) for local ordinances
  • Google Civic Information API to look up a user's actual representatives by address
